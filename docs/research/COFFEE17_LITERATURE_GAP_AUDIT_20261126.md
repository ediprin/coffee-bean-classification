# Coffee17 literature and research-gap audit — 2026-11-26

Status: LITERATURE_AUDIT / NO_NEW_TRAINING. ConvNeXt V2 Nano remains HOLD as a proposed thesis method. This audit is targeted, not a systematic review guaranteeing completeness. Searches used Coffee17 dataset slug, original DOI, author, 979/6853 counts, defect classification and backbone synonyms; inspected primary journal text and relevant supplied PDFs. Web PDF screenshots failed for Kaewkamol and Gunawan; tables and prose readable as extracted text, graphical-only model variants not exhaustively verified.

## Same dataset, distinguish endpoint
- Arwatchananukul et al. (2024), Smart Agricultural Technology, DOI 10.1016/j.atech.2024.100680. Original 979 images; 6853 after rotations. Five families MobileNetV2/V3, EfficientNetV2, InceptionV2, ResNetV2. Table 9 validation 84.87/90.19/78.61/73.60/76.42%. Table 11 validation CV 98.78–99.84%. Table 12 test accuracy 88.63%, Macro F1 89.04%, support 686 (not 979 unseen original beans); Partial Sour F1 77.78%, Slight Insect Damage 78.38%; authors identify similar defects as remaining challenge. Section 3 describes augmentation before 70/20/10 partition, no documented original identity grouping found. Do not assert confirmed leakage or compare scores directly with deduplicated OOF. Also pruning/quantization studied.
- Kaewkamol et al. (2025), Automated Defect Classification of Coffee Beans Using Deep-Stacking Ensemble Learning, https://ph01.tci-thaijo.org/index.php/IJSIT/article/view/260481 . Same 17-class data, 6853 after rotation; 70/20/10 split described afterward, grouping unspecified. 20 standard models and 14 lightweight; VGG, ResNet, EfficientNet, ConvNeXt, RegNet, MobileNet, TinyNet, LCNet, MNASNet, RepGhost. Table 2 includes Haralick and DWT; Table 3 ConvNeXt-Tiny example. ConvNeXt 72.94% test accuracy, LCNet 29.41%, probability-vector SVM stacking 87.64%. Meta-training provenance/OOF independence not sufficiently documented in extracted methods. Full list and exact best weight variant in graphical figures not visually verified. Generic backbone comparison, DWT as handcrafted extraction, and CNN/SVM stacking are not new to this dataset.
- Gunawan & Udjulawa (2026), DOI 10.35889/progresif.v22i1.3344. Coffee17 subset 558 images/10 classes; 80/20 split, describes train-only augmentation; ResNet-50 feature extraction and RBF SVM, 64.29% CNN to 77.68% CNN-SVM accuracy, F1 77.47%. Not 17-class comparator.
- Grading of Specialty-Grade Coffea arabica Beans Using Digital Imaging and Machine Learning (2026), DOI 10.1007/s12161-025-02961-1. Uses 979 Coffee17 defective images and 521 specialty images as external binary evaluation. Custom CNN 100%, MobileNetV2 99.7%, MobileNetV3 99.3%. Not subtype classification.
- Hasanudin (2026), USK thesis index, Klasifikasi Biji Kopi Menggunakan Multi-Dataset pada Edge-AI. Uses USK-Coffee and Coffee17 for binary classification; full thesis not accessed.
- Public non-peer-reviewed repo https://github.com/AlexHuc/coffee-quality-inspection-vision reports ConvNeXt Tiny/EfficientNet/MobileNet/ResNet on original dataset. Not an academic benchmark; further evidence that a backbone switch alone is not original.

## Related domain papers, different endpoints
- Sandhya et al. 2026, DOI 10.1007/s44196-026-01377-x, Deep-CofeNet, 8000 front-view images, 48000 augmented, 5 labels (good, sour, immature, insect bite, withered); CBI-GAN synthetic views and DA-MobileNet; 98.96% reported; states instance-level split 75/25; actual table variable synthesis counts vs stated 1:3 recipe, Data Availability says no datasets generated/analyzed, source provenance unclear. Not Coffee17.
- Jiao et al. 2025, DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0322198, Swin-HSSAM; 10378 own images, four grade labels (three normal grades + defects grouped, nine defect subtypes); public gpiosenka dataset, not Coffee17; 96.50% average accuracy and 96.48% F1 on that public dataset; selective attention and fusion loss.
- Hu et al. 2025, DOI 10.1016/j.lwt.2025.118631, Siamese learning 3220 images/7 categories, reports 94.95%; pair classification/defect assessment, not equivalent to 17-class closed set; describes pair-level partition, individual-image overlap not established, on-request data.
- Chang & Liu 2024, DOI 10.1109/ACCESS.2024.3356596, multiscale defect extraction, 7300 training/validation data, 96% overall, own data. Hsia et al. 2022 DOI 10.3390/app122110966 lightweight DSC/SE/skip, optimizer RA/LA/GC, LIME, reported 98.38 accuracy. Muchtar et al. 2025 USK-Coffee binary, benchmarks ConvNeXt, FocalNet, ViT/Swin and CNNs, Grad-CAM Jetson. Ke et al. 2025 DOI via https://www.mdpi.com/2673-4591/92/1/69 DWP transformer, Coffee Cobra binary and 4 roasting levels. Vachmanus et al. 2026 AL-ViT, 2098 Robusta images binary, fixed 420 test, ViT B/16 and active selection. Febriana 2022 USK four classes; Hassan 2024 different gpiosenka dataset; Hashmi 2025 four-class USK-type taxonomy. Do not claim attention, multiscale, transformer, active learning, contrastive/Siamese or XAI are absent in coffee research.

## Independent data
Kesiman et al. 2023 DOI 10.1109/ICITRI59340.2023.10249345: 8135 image samples overall, 17 nominal defect labels under SNI but Table II small husk empty; 53.35% best 17-class benchmark. No open downloadable dataset verified. Auliya et al. Mendeley data DOI 10.17632/ffhdwp4428.1 is a segmentation dataset; paper counterpart and actual annotations not verified. Need source-scene grouping, class mapping before using crops. Neither established as independent 17-class equivalent. USK and Robusta binary cannot establish 17-class generalization.

## Research implications
Direct gaps in inspected 17-class papers: explicit original-identity-separated augmentation/evaluation, single-model robust fine-grained gains beyond standalone CNN and probability stacking, class-level evaluation of persistent errors under matched split. Not proof that no other paper has addressed these; methodology gap alone does not meet user's performance-improvement objective. B0 86.849% pooled internal Macro F1, 87.047% accuracy, C2 gate failed; prototype frozen K2 failed. No independent data claim. 
Recommended next controlled research step: establish one accuracy-first literature-backed reference, ConvNeXt Tiny v1 (already used by Kaewkamol, use clearly pinned weights and matched development protocol; not exact reproduction unless paper settings verified). Consider ConvNeXt V2 Nano as alternative later only if scientifically justified; architecture swap is baseline, not novelty. Do not screen dozens of backbones. Any new training requires explicit frozen contract, no test tuning, no retrain B0 merely to retrieve artifacts, preserve checkpoints. If proposing an actual training-method contribution, prioritize an end-to-end discriminative or generalization objective distinct from frozen prototypes and previously failed GCE/focal/DCL; prior-art audit and matched ablation before implementation. Coffee17 later tests remain retrospective internal. No model selected or trained in this audit.
