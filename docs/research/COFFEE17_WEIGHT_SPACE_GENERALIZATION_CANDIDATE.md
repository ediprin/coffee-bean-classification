## Literature
Foret et al., Sharpness-Aware Minimization for Efficiently Improving Generalization, ICLR 2021, https://openreview.net/forum?id=6Tm1mposlrM . Table 3 page 7: EfficientNet-B7 top-1 test error without/with SAM on Aircraft 8.15/6.80%, Flowers 1.16/0.63%, Cars 5.94/5.18%, Pets 4.24/3.97%. These are not Coffee17 nor Macro-F1; larger model and training settings differ. Appendix C.3 gives fixed rho=.05 experiments; not an optimized Coffee17 rho. Hsia et al. 2022 DOI 10.3390/app122110966 already studies RAdam/Lookahead/Gradient Centralization in coffee: optimizer improvements alone are not a novelty claim. SAM is not identical to those optimizers.
Prior-art restrictions: Focal-SAM ICML 2025 https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/li25dk.html already uses class-wise sharpness penalties; layer-wise perturbation scaling NeurIPS 2024 https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/449a016a6ce6fba3fe50d05482abf836-Abstract-Conference.html ; Friendly SAM CVPR 2024 already addresses perturbation gradient; do not claim class weighting/layer scaling as new. L2-SP Li et al. ICML 2018 https://proceedings.mlr.press/v80/li18a.html ; Yang et al. ICCV 2023 Concept-wise Fine-tuning documents possible negative transfer from strict anchoring, so no automatic L2-SP combination. Searches found no clear direct SAM study on Coffee17 but do not assert first.
